Information about personnel-shift

 

declare=mfdc=ue_api_time

mfdc=personnel=load=1

mfdc=personnel=loadshift=<today,yyyy/mm/dd>

messagebox=Current Shift <mfdc.shift.shift> <mfdc.shift.shiftdescription>

After this declaration you've follow Variables for object "shift"

 

 

shift.shift

Current shift of the User

Example:

messagebox=Current Shift <mfdc.shift.shift>

shift.shiftdescription

Description of shift

shift.begin1

Start shift

shift.begin2

Start shift 2

shift.break1from, shift.break1to

Begin and end from break1

shift.break2from, shift.break2to

Begin and end from break2

shift.break3from, shift.break3to

Begin and end from break3

shift.end1, shift.end2

Start and Start-End of shift

shift.planduration1

Planned hours for this day  (field tag_std)

shift.planduration2

Planned hours (second entry) for this day (field tag_std2)

shift.planduration3

Planned hours (third entry) for this day (field tag_std3)
