RecalculateCalendar


Contents

Recalculates the Resource calendar

 

ResourceCalendar/RecalculateCalendar(ResourceId,DateFrom,DateTo)

 

ResourceId and DateFrom are mandatory

DateTo can be empty. In this case Resource is recalculated until last entry in Calendar

 

Example:

recalculate resource 1002 from 2021-11-20 to 2021-11-30

ResourceCalendar/RecalculateCalendar("1002","2021-11-20,"2021-11-30")

 

recalculate resource 1002 from 2021-11-20 up to the last entry in the calendar

ResourceCalendar/RecalculateCalendar("1002","2021-11-20,"")
