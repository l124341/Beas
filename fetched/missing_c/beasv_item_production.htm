BEASV_ITEM_PRODUCTION


Contents

The BEASV_ITEM_PRODUCTION dashboard view contains all the production details of all items by date. The view contains both the goods receipt and the goods issue, the item details, the quantity, units, the shipment date, the line total, the unit price and their respective currency.
