BEASV_RESOURCE_UTILIZATION


Contents

The BEASV_RESOURCE_UTILIZATION dashboard view contains the planned capacity, actual utilization and the interruption duration in hours for all resources per day. The actual utilization is the total of the working hours during the day for the resource, and the interruption duration is the interruption hours in the working schedule of the resource.
