BEASV_WORKORDER_BOM


Contents

 

Displays all work order Bill of materials positions.

 

Link: BEASV_WORKORDER -> BEASV_WORKORDER_POS -> BEASV_WORKORDER_BOM

 

In the BoM there are the linenumber2 (internal number), the Position (visible Line number) and the sort (Sort of bill of Material).
